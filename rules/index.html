Site Rules


BY USING THIS SITE, YOU AGREE TO FOLLOWING THE RULES. IF CAUGHT BREAKING THESE RULES, THE DIAMOND TEAM CAN ACT IN ANY WAY THEY DEEM FIT:



1. Please respect the forum's user-base and its mod-team: We as a community do not condone any form of harassment or discrimination here at all: racism, homophobia, sexism, death threats, stalking etc. If you believe a user is breaching the conditions of this rule, please report it to one of the staff so we can deal with it appropriately.
2. Quality of posts here do matter: Please do not spam the forums for the sake of gaining reputation or getting your post noticed; it will result in a seven day mute. This includes: begging for certain mods to be added to the server, spam content, unnecessary pings towards staff and low effort posts that are clearly rep-fishing.
3. No advertisement of any kind: Do not use your posts to advertise any sort of product that can be bought, off-site social media and other related content that could potentially scam your fellow user.
4. Report anything that seems suspicious, malicious or strange: Make sure you report anything to the moderation team that seems to be against the rules. We as a team have a responsibility to keep you all safe and content, so you reporting any issues you come across does heavily help with that.
5. Do not engage in illegal activity: The forum’s servers are based in the Netherlands, so Netherlands law should be followed at all times regarding what you post.
6. No sexual-content: Under no circumstances will you post anything that is mildly NSFW or explicitly so. If you do, it will result in a permanent ban from the forum which is unappealable. We have a community consisting of both children and adults, so in order to keep everyone safe, it is an outright ban on such content.
7. No purposeful flaming or trolling: We understand that our community likes to indulge in some casual banter, but please do not go out of your way to cause arguments or instigate trolling campaigns towards others.
8. Do not join the site if you are under 13: We completely get that Minecraft is a game for everyone, however, we wish to keep those who are too young to understand forum culture safe. If you are caught bypassing this rule, you will be banned from the site.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WARNINGS, MUTES AND BANS: When a rule is broken, it will be up to the moderator or administrator issuing the punishment what exactly you will get. In most cases, you will get a warning via a comment beneath the post that has violated our rules explaining what you have done and a polite request to not do it again. If you continue to break the rules, however, we will issue a mute (duration can vary) and then eventually a ban. If you wish to appeal, please contact one of the staff members directly.
SUPPORT: Any questions regarding our rules should be directed towards the forum team, we will try our best to get back to you quickly.
