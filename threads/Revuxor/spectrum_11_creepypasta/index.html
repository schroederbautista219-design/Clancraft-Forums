This is a collection of dates with events since the beginning of my investigation until the most recent at the date this forum was made. Thus dates here will predate the forum.


13-10-2010
As a 16 yo I was very lucky to be able to visit the location where I would eventually go to college. As a field trip we all took a trip to Kennedy Space Center in Florida. My English was a little spotty at the time so I mainly kept to myself and other students. It was an amazing experience. One of my favorite public locations was the Rocket Garden. The visit itself isn’t the important part but I ended up attending the Florida Institute of Technology 2 years later as an international student.


I think I must’ve spent 2+ hours looking at the images taken from the Hubble Space Telescope. Seeing all the beautiful galaxies, stars, nebulae, etc. But one thing of interest did catch my eye. Later in the evening when at the hotel, I came across an uploaded image of the satellite itself. This shouldn’t be possible as photos are uploaded directly from the cameras in the device, not a 3rd person view. But that isn’t even the craziest thing. The Image itself consisted of a mass of black and red biomass covering the entire telescope. The image was deleted from public records within the hour but I managed to save an image right before. I think NASA got concerned cause they had another satellite take an image of the telescope shortly after but nothing was there. No traces of what I had seen. It was as if the thing on the telescope knew it was being watched, and left all traces of it gone.

21-10-2010
For connections I will list as we go on, this “creepypasta” has connections to the thing on the HST. Anyways, I came across a forum where a bunch of people found a pirated copy of an alpha minecraft version (1.1.2_01). Yes I play minecraft, shocking. Anyways people have been reporting a strange new entity in the game and have been comparing it to older creepypastas. I like mysteries so I decided to play for a little bit.


It was pretty much the average run of the mill cursed hacked mc jar. This is what I would’ve continued to think had I not encountered the entity everyone was talking about. A few days in, the fog closed in and locked my render distance and a tall black skinny figure with a triangle as a head was in the fog, stalking me. Yeah, what a bullshit entity. Not even scary. I approached it and saw what it was made of…… The exact same black material as the thing on the telescope. When angered, it will open multiple red eyes. I have attached an image to show similarities. Not enough conclusive evidence but this thing definitely looks familiar. Almost like it intended to get into the game.

16-07-2013
This post is not related to Icarus or Aspect’s posts and is part of my own curiosity of where this guy could’ve come from. I keep hearing mentions of the “Void” in my head. Whenever I play this game, it’s like something is whispering secrets I shouldn’t know. Trying to scare me. But as someone who is in college focusing on space, I have to think of a much scarier question. What is the void. Is it an entity? An object? An idea?


Astronomers usually refer to the “void” as little spots of nothingness in space. Areas where the universe is dark. I’ve mapped out all locations of where “voids” are located. But I don’t even know if that’s what the voices are even referring to. It could just mean “the absence of something” and not a physical place. Another connection to the void I maybe could see is the entity itself. It’s mostly black. The void usually means nothing. Black is associated with the absence of light, despite technically containing all colors of the visible light spectrum. Void also means zero in other languages. A lot of signs keep pointing to the fact that the void simply just means “the unknown”. I don’t think whatever this entity is wants us to know where it comes from. I am grasping at ideas but I feel if I don’t make sense of it now, I will go crazy in the end.
