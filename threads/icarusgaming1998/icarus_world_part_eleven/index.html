The void holes in my world have continued to surround my house without a sign of stopping. Admittedly, it’s becoming harder and harder to traverse my world without accidentally falling into one. It’s strange, really. My heart pounds every single damned time I misclick and slip, my skin erupting into hives²⁵ without fail. It’s as though my body believes I am in danger, despite nothing actually being there. Never before have I reacted so viscerally to something as simple as dying in a video game.


I know Revuxor and Aspect have been trying to convince me to stop playing. They think I’ve clearly been at this for far too long and that it’s time to take a break, but. I don’t know. This is the first time I’ve accepted that I might be experiencing a genuine anomaly within a video game, rather than some fictional sludge somebody has cobbled together.


If I pursue this, I could write something /huge/. Something genuinely authentic instead of my usual, crappy debunking threads.
